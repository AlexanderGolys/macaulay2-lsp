# projectiveHilbertPolynomial

**projectiveHilbertPolynomial  -- Hilbert polynomial of projective space**

**Type:** [MethodFunction](../Type/MethodFunction.mdx)

## Description

`projectiveHilbertPolynomial n`  -- produces the projective Hilbert polynomial corresponding to projective space of dimension n.`projectiveHilbertPolynomial(nd)`  -- produces the projective Hilbert polynomial corresponding to the graded ring of projective space of dimension n but with its generator in degree -d.
