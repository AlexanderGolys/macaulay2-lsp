# log1p

**log1p  -- logarithm of 1+x**

log1p  -- logarithm of 1+x

## Additional Information

- Inputs:
- `x` a [ complex number](../Type/op_x226_x132_x130.mdx) or a [ real number](../Type/op_x226_x132_x157.mdx)
- `I` a [ real interval](../Type/RRi.mdx)
- Outputs:
- a [ real number](../Type/op_x226_x132_x157.mdx) or a [ complex number](../Type/op_x226_x132_x130.mdx) the logarithm of `1+x`
- a [ real interval](../Type/RRi.mdx) an interval containing logarithm of 1 plus the points of `I`
