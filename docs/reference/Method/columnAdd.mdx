## Additional Information

- Inputs:
- `m` a [ mutable matrix](../Type/op_Coredollar_MutableMatrix.mdx)
- `i` an [ integer](../Type/ZZ.mdx)
- `a` a [ ring element](../Type/RingElement.mdx) in the same ring as `m`
- `j` an [ integer](../Type/ZZ.mdx)
- Consequences:
- The `i` th column of `m` is modified by adding `a` times the `j` th column of `m`
